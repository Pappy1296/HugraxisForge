About the Game

HugraxisForge is a game built around challenges, progression, deliberate practice, and building improved concentration.

Instead of simply running independent training sessions, the game puts the player into a progression system that adapts to the player's development.

Concentration forms an important foundation of the experience, while challenges and practice provide opportunities for the player to develop and improve.

Core Experience

Challenges

Solve problems and overcome challenges while building memory, concentration, and retention through engaging gameplay.

Progression

Develop your abilities through rewards, variation, and increasingly demanding challenges.

Deliberate Practice

Mistakes are part of the learning process. HugraxisForge is designed around identifying weaknesses and practicing them.
