About the Game

HugraxisForge is a game built around challenges, progression, deliberate practice, and building improved concentration.

Instead of simply running independent n-back sessions, the game puts the player in a progression system that adapts to the player development. It focus heavily on building concentration as the foundation of development.

Core Experience

Challenges

Solve problems and overcome challenges that test understanding, reasoning, and practical thinking.

Progression

Build knowledge and ability through continued practice and increasingly demanding challenges.

Deliberate Practice

Mistakes are part of the learning process. HugraxisForge is designed around identifying weaknesses and practicing them.
